What Excelra Does

Excelra is a leading global life sciences data and AI company that transforms complex scientific data into actionable insights — empowering pharmaceutical and biotech companies to accelerate drug discovery and improve patient outcomes. The company combines deep expertise in chemistry, biology, clinical sciences, and data engineering to support every phase of the drug development lifecycle, from early discovery through to regulatory submission.

By integrating proprietary databases, scientific informatics platforms, and AI-driven analytics, Excelra helps organisations reduce attrition, modernise legacy lab systems, and bring new therapies to patients faster. The team's hybrid expertise — scientific domain knowledge paired with modern data engineering — is what makes the company a long-term partner rather than a transactional vendor.

Flagship Products

GS
GOSTAR®
Global Online Structure-Activity Relationship Database — the world's largest curated small molecule drug discovery database, covering over 10.6 million chemical structures, 35 million bioactivities, and 79,000 biological targets across SAR, ADMET, preclinical, clinical, and structural data.
GB
GOBIOM™
Comprehensive biomarker repository covering preclinical, clinical, and exploratory phases — enabling biomarker discovery and validation at scale across therapeutic areas.
CT
CTOD
Clinical Trial Outcome Database — supporting MBMA (Model-Based Meta-Analysis) and clinical data analysis across therapeutic areas, powering cross-trial benchmarking and probability-of-success modelling.
